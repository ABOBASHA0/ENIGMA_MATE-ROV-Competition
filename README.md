#What is the MATE ROV Competition?

he MATE ROV Competition uses underwater robotics (aka remotely operated vehicles or ROVs) to inspire and challenge students to learn and creatively
apply scientific, engineering, and technical skills to solving real-world problems. This competition challenges K-12, community college,
and university students from all over the world to tackle missions based on scenarios from the workplace, encouraging students to work together,
network, and learn from technical professionals and each other.
